How to read it: all numbers are % per leg (one $500 position). "Per day" adds up everything one desk does in a day: one 24h trade, or three 8h trades, each paying the round-trip cost. On a $1,000 desk with ten $500 legs, 0.10% per leg per day = $5 a day. Costs: 0.11% per round trip (taker both ways) and 0.04% (maker both ways).
