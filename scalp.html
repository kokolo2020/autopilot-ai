The question: can a quick trade win often enough to pay its fees? Every 15 minutes the lab opens two entries: Momentum (the coin whose 15-minute and 60-minute moves agree most strongly, traded in that direction) and Random (a random coin and side, the no-edge control). Each entry runs in four take-profit / stop-loss sizes. $500 per trade, 24h time limit, paper money.

Break-even win % = (stop + round-trip fee) / (take profit + stop). Taker fees are 0.11% round trip (OKX 0.055% per side); maker is 0.04% (0.02% per side, limit orders). A coin flip wins stop / (take profit + stop) of the time, so the random rows should sit near that line and lose about the fee. A setup only counts as an edge when its win % stays above break-even with 95% confidence (the "low" column).
