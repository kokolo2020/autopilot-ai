How to read this

  • It is pretend money. The strategies place no real orders. Prices are live, fees (0.055% per side) are charged, but real trades would also pay slippage and could be liquidated, which this test does not model.
  • A few days prove very little. A strategy that looks great for a week can stop working the moment the market changes. We mark each one "too early to judge" until it has at least 150 closed trades over 7 or more days.
  • Gains and losses are shown in dollars and as a share of the $12,500 pretend bank each strategy starts with. Several use 5x leverage, so swings are bigger than the coins' own moves.
  • Nothing here is financial advice. More experimental strategies are tested on the research dashboard.
