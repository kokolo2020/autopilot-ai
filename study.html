History study: bears, bulls and mean reversion

Does "buy yesterday's weakest, short yesterday's strongest" hold up, and does the best long/short split change with the market phase? Daily OKX history for the major coins. Nothing here trades.
Press the button. It downloads about 5 years of daily candles for about 40 coins (1 to 2 minutes), then everything is calculated in your browser.

Caveats, so we do not over-trust it: (1) the coin list is today's survivors, which flatters "buy the loser" because coins that died are missing; (2) daily candles close at 00:00 UTC (07:00 Bangkok), not 20:00; (3) these are spot prices, so no funding or perpetual quirks; (4) costs are shown at 0.11% (taker both ways), 0.075% and 0.04% per leg round trip; (5) one cycle is a handful of independent episodes, not thousands. 8-hour hold study · Back to research
