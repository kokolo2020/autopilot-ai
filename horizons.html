AUTOPILOT HORIZONS NEW · Rev 3 · paper money

One strategy, four time horizons. Each batch goes long the 8 weakest and short the 8 strongest coins (24h change) with $250 margin per leg, starts at 4x, and every entry carries a $25 stop (10% of margin). A stopped leg reopens on the same side at 5x, then 6x, then 7x, then it is done. Whatever is left open is closed at the horizon. Research dashboard · Public summary
Back-test (42 overlapping 24h batches, one falling market) favoured this rule. The weekly, monthly and yearly versions are untested guesses. A $25 stop is a tight stop over a long horizon, so expect legs to finish early and the long horizons to behave very differently from the daily one. Funding payments are not modelled. Paper only, nothing here places real orders.
