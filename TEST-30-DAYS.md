# 30-day test: Neutral Rev (written 17 Oct 2026, before the results)

Rules fixed in advance so we can't move the goalposts.

- Test: one batch per day, 30 batches. Batch 1 opened 15 Oct 2026, batch 30 closes about 14 Nov.
- Strategy: short the 8 strongest, long the 8 weakest coins (24h change), equal size, 5x, closed after 24h. No changes during the test. If we change anything, the 30 days restart.
- All numbers are net after fees, paper money.

## Pass (all four must hold)
1. Total net P&L after 30 batches is at least +$1,000.
2. At least 18 of 30 batches are positive.
3. No single batch is worse than -$500.
4. Biggest drop from a peak is no more than $1,000.

## Fail
Anything else. Say so in the Day 30 post, and say which rule failed.
