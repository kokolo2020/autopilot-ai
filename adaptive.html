Round 3: strategies that switch with the market

Each day at the 07:00 close the market is put into one of four regimes (Bull, Bear, Chop, Panic) from BTC's trend and breadth. Every candidate strategy is tested inside every regime, then a switching system picks one strategy per regime. The rules are chosen on the years up to 2023 (2022 and 2023, after a 200-day warm-up) and checked on 2024 to 2026, which they never saw. Daily OKX history, about 40 coins. Nothing here trades.
Press the button. Downloads about 5 years of daily candles (1 to 2 minutes, cached 12 hours, shared with the History study), then calculates in your browser.

Rules, fixed before the test. Regime at each close: Panic = the average coin fell 3% or more that day; Panic lasts that day and the 2 days after (the dip-buy hold), and overrides the other regimes. Bull = BTC above its 200-day average, BTC up over 30 days, and at least half the coins above their own 50-day average. Bear = BTC below its 200-day average, BTC down over 30 days, and fewer than half above their 50-day average. Otherwise Chop. A Bull/Bear/Chop change only counts after 3 days in a row. Money: one $1,000 desk trading $5,000 of positions (5x, like the live desks), fees on every change of position, no funding (spot history), decisions use only data up to that close.
